Slick Business Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Last updated 18 October 2026 (ads and usage statistics)

This policy explains what information the mobile game Slick Business ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by ChillyGamesDev ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email chillygamesdev@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy
A player ID, created automatically when you first play (you start as a guest)To back up your game, run the leaderboard and restore purchases
Username and password, only if you choose to create an accountSo you can sign in on another phone. Passwords are handled by Unity Authentication and are never visible to us
Game progress (cash, pumps, research, Bonds, items and settings saved by the game)Cloud save, so your progress is not lost
Leaderboard name and score (your all-time earnings in the game)To show the "Richest Oil Barons" leaderboard. Your leaderboard name and score are visible to other players
Records of in-app purchases (which items you own)To give you what you bought and restore one-time purchases
Advertising ID, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions, collected by Google AdMob once ad consent allows it (the game keeps one rewarded video loaded in the background)To show the optional rewarded videos, measure them and prevent ad fraud
Gameplay statistics (for example sessions, play time, how far you progress and your device model), only if you agree to share usage statsTo understand how the game is played and what to improve. Never used for advertising
Basic technical data (for example an installation ID and service diagnostics)Sent by the Unity services the game uses, to keep them working and secure

We do not collect your real name, precise location, contacts, photos or files, and we do not sell your information.

Usage statistics

On first launch the game asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is collected unless you say yes. They are collected by Unity Analytics and are not used for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Share usage stats; turning it off also asks Unity to delete what was shared.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos: you can choose to watch an ad in return for an in-game bonus. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one, and the Ad-Free Pass lets you skip the videos entirely. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you are in the UK, the EU or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your choice before any ads load, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Payments

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Services we use

Ads come from Google AdMob (see Advertising above and the Google Privacy Policy).

The game uses Unity Gaming Services from Unity Technologies (Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Analytics and In-App Purchasing) to store and process the information above for us. See the Unity Privacy Policy. Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have a player profile. If you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, it is removed from Unity's services, usually within 30 days. Progress saved only on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or reset your progress in Settings.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all of the data linked to it at any time:

  • In the game: Settings → Cloud save → Delete account (shown as Delete cloud data if you play as a guest).
  • By email: send a request to chillygamesdev@gmail.com with the subject "Slick Business account deletion", including your username (or your leaderboard name if you play as a guest). We will confirm when it's done.

Deleting your account removes your cloud save, your leaderboard entry and your username. Purchases you made stay in your Google Play order history, which Google controls.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the UK or EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it is used. Email chillygamesdev@gmail.com to make a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
